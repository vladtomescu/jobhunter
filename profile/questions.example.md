# Questions for the first call

Pick 3 to 5 per kit. Whatever resolves a blocking unknown from the score comes first. Adapt the wording to the posting and keep the substance. Drop any question the posting already answers, and put a concrete question about this role in its place, written in the same register.

This is the example question bank that ships with JobHunter. Copy it to `profile/questions.md` under your data root and adapt the bank to your own contract form and work authorization.

## The bank

1. Is this a direct engagement with the company, or through your agency, and who is the end client?
2. Is the role offered as employment, as a contractor engagement, or either?
3. What does the payments or ledger work concretely involve day to day?
4. How large is the team, and who owns technical direction?
5. What is the compensation range for this level, and what are the remote and timezone expectations?
6. What does the process look like, and how many stages are there?

## Blocking unknowns map to questions

- `end_client`: question 1.
- `b2b`: question 2.
- `timezone`: the remote and timezone half of question 5, asked on its own when compensation is already stated.
- `us_authorization`: ask plainly whether the role requires US work authorization, and state my own work authorization as the profile's Standard answers section gives it.

## Rules

- Question 5 asks for their range. I never state a figure of my own, in a question or anywhere else.
- Never ask something the posting already answers. It reads as not having read it.
- Neutral tone, no eagerness, no flattery. These are the questions of someone who is choosing, not applying under pressure.
- One sentence each, no preamble around them.
